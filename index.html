Meet Flor

An analytical perspective, grounded in people.

Flor Behrouzshad is a Licensed Real Estate Agent and Licensed Professional Engineer (P.Eng.) who begins by understanding the client — their priorities, capabilities, circumstances, and vision for the future.

A graduate of Sharif University of Technology, Flor brings structure and disciplined analysis to her work through her engineering background. Her understanding of economics, policy, business, and design adds context. Together, they support a personalized strategy for residential, investment, commercial, and business decisions.

Her perspective is informed by over a decade of experience across engineering, property projects, design, and value creation.

Supported by brokerage resources and professionals across relevant disciplines.
