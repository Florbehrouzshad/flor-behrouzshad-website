Buying

A clear purchase begins before the property search.

The buying process starts with the client: how they want to live, what feels financially comfortable, the timeline they are working with, and what they want the property to do for them over time.

The Approach

Define the decision

Clarify priorities, budget, timing, location preferences, property type, and longer-term plans.

Read the market

Review neighbourhood conditions, comparable sales, supply, competition, and the factors affecting value.

Evaluate the property

Look beyond presentation to layout, condition, location, carrying costs, resale considerations, and future potential.

Build the offer strategy

Structure price, conditions, timing, and negotiation around the property, the market, and the client’s risk tolerance.

Move toward closing

Coordinate the transaction through conditions, due diligence, financing, legal steps, and closing preparation.
